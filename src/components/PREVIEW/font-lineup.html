What you are matching — verified from the screenshot

  • Single vertical bar, interrupted — hidden where it crosses the S body; visible only as stubs above the top terminal and below the bottom terminal.
  • Stubs extend roughly 10–20% of glyph height beyond cap line and baseline — not tiny nubs.
  • Monolinear — the bar is the same stroke weight as the digits.
  • S terminals cut on the diagonal, following the curve’s exit angle.
  • Digits: lining, proportional; flat straight base on 2, flat top bar on 5, open apertures.
  • Ruled out: any font whose bar crosses fully through the S counters (Roboto, SF Pro, Inter, Helvetica).
